When autonomous systems deploy at scale, they interact with large-scale transportation and infrastructure systems. We study how autonomous agents affect and integrate into these networks through **cooperative autonomous driving** via language communication, **infrastructure-aware autonomy**, and **system-level optimization** for safety and efficiency in human-AI mobility systems.
